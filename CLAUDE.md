# the-daily-chase

## Personal writing

Chase's own writing (Mariemont Town Crier pieces, civic letters, personal
emails) lives in `writing/`, a Compound Writing home (`VOICE.md`, `STYLE.md`,
`examples/`, `drafts/`). Read `writing/CLAUDE.md` before drafting or editing
any of it. (The old 75/25 authorship rule was dropped on Nov 1, 2026.)
The `compound-writing` plugin is enabled in `.claude/settings.json`.
