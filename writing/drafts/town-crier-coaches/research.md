## Erik becomes Mariemont's winningest coach (Aug 20, 2026, at Ross)

- **Source of claim:** Lisa Vanags (Erik's wife), told to Chase, Oct 2026.
- **Game:** **Verified.** Mariemont won 6-0 at Ross on Thursday, Aug 20, 2026 (7:15 PM, away). It was the second win of the 2026 season, after a 3-0 win at Seven Hills on Aug 14. [MaxPreps schedule](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/cincinnati/mariemont-warriors/soccer/schedule/)
- **"Winningest coach in Mariemont history":** **Unverified.** No public program record book or news story found. Need the previous record holder and his total from the athletic department.
- **Erik's win total (MaxPreps, partial):** 194 wins over the 2012 to 2025 seasons, so the Ross game was at least win **196**. [MaxPreps history](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/cincinnati/mariemont-warriors/soccer/history/)

## The Ross game was Erik's 200th win (Suzy call, Oct 15)

- **Source of claim:** Lisa Vanags, relayed by Suzy Weinland.
- **Status:** **Partly verified.** If the Ross game was win 200, Erik had 198 wins before the 2026 season. MaxPreps credits him with 194 from 2012 to 2025, leaving **4 wins unaccounted for**. Possible explanations:
  - Seasons MaxPreps is missing (2010 and 2011 show as blank).
  - Games MaxPreps never recorded.
  - A count that includes his years at Milford or as an assistant. The [staff bio](https://mhswarriors.com/main/staff/id/18362/seasonid/35115/staffid/25833) mentions a 38-18-10 record with teams he sent up to varsity.
- **Ask Erik or the athletic director:** is 200 his Mariemont head-coach total, and when did it start?
